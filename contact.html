FAQ

Questions owners usually ask first.

What businesses do you help?

Short-term rental hosts first, then local service businesses, cleaning companies, restaurants, gyms, salons, and small retail stores.

Do I need clean data?

No. Clean data helps, but part of the process is organizing messy booking, expense, sales, or spreadsheet data.

Is my data private?

Yes. Business data should only be used for the report and should not be shared publicly without permission.

How long does it take?

Most one-time reports are designed for a 48–72 hour turnaround after required data is received.

What do I receive?

A PDF or Notion-style report with key insights, charts, recommendations, and a 30-day action plan.

Do you offer monthly support?

Yes, but only after the first report shows ongoing tracking would be useful.
